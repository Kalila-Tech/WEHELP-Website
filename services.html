Our Services

Support, whenever you need it.

24/7 Text Line Support

Our confidential text line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can reach out and speak to someone whenever you need support, guidance, or simply a listening ear.

24/7 Support Hotline

Immediate access to trained support staff who are ready to listen, offer encouragement, and help you navigate difficult moments at any time of the day or night.

Therapy Support

We connect individuals with professional therapy services and mental health resources that provide ongoing care, guidance, and support tailored to their needs.

Support should never have an opening or closing time. No matter when you reach out, someone is there to listen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our text line, hotline and support.

Do I need a diagnosis before I contact WE HELP?

No. No diagnosis is required and no problem is too small. You can simply reach out and be honest about what you are carrying.

Is the text line available at night and on weekends?

Yes. Our text line and support hotline are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Our text line is a confidential, judgment-free space. If you are in immediate danger, call 112, Police 10111 or Ambulance 10177 right away.

Can WE HELP replace therapy?

No. Peer support and listening are not a replacement for professional care. We connect people to professional therapy services and mental health resources for ongoing support.

How can I volunteer or sponsor WE HELP?

Use our enquiry form to ask about volunteering or sponsoring and you will get an instant response with the next steps.
