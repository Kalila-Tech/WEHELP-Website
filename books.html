Recommended Reading

A few starting points — not a substitute for professional care.

David D. Burns

Feeling Good: The New Mood Therapy

A widely used introduction to cognitive-behavioral techniques for working with anxious and depressive thinking patterns.

Bessel van der Kolk

The Body Keeps the Score

Explores how trauma reshapes the body and mind, and surveys approaches used in trauma recovery.

Matt Haig

Reasons to Stay Alive

A memoir of living through severe depression and anxiety, and finding a way back toward hope.

Johann Hari

Lost Connections

Looks beyond individual biology to the social and environmental roots of depression and anxiety.

Megan Devine

It's OK That You're Not OK

A compassionate guide to grief that pushes back on pressure to "move on" quickly.

Brené Brown

The Gifts of Imperfection

An accessible guide to self-compassion, vulnerability, and letting go of who you think you're supposed to be.
