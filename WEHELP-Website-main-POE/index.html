Welcome to WE HELP

We are here to provide support and assistance to those in need. Our mission is to make a positive impact in the community.

HELP exists so that no one has to face a mental health struggle in silence.

We provide a safe, judgment free space where people can speak honestly about what they're going through — and be met with someone who listens, day or night.

Through our 24/7 text line, support hotline, and connections to professional therapy, we meet people wherever they are in their struggle and walk with them toward help, healing, and hope.

Remember that you are never alone. You have us.

What We Provide: 24/7 Mental Health Support

  • 24/7 Text Line Support

    A confidential, judgment free space to reach out by text — any hour, any day. For people who aren't ready to talk out loud, or simply feel safer writing things down, this is a quiet way to say "I need someone" without having to explain everything at once.

    How the text line works

  • 24/7 Support Hotline

    Immediate access to a trained, caring voice on the other end of the phone. Whether it's a quiet moment of anxiety or an overwhelming crisis, our hotline exists so that no one has to sit with a hard moment completely alone.

    Call the hotline

  • Therapy Support

    We connect individuals to professional therapy services and mental health resources that can provide ongoing care, guidance, and support. Support should never have an opening or closing time no matter when you reach out, someone is there to listen.

    Ask for a therapy referral

  • A Judgment-Free Space to Be Heard

    Beyond crisis response, WE HELP offers something simple but often hard to find: someone who will genuinely listen. No diagnosis required, no problem too small — just a space to be honest about what you're carrying.

    About our mission

  • Resources and Guidance

    Information and direction for people who aren't sure where to start — helping them recognize what they're feeling, understand their options, and take the next step toward support, at their own pace.

    Browse recommended reading

  • A Reminder That Help Is Possible

    Perhaps most importantly, WE HELP provides reassurance — to someone who feels like they've run out of options — that support exists, healing is possible, and they are not a burden for asking.

    Contact us today

  • Peers and Community

    A space to connect with others who've faced similar struggles — not as a replacement for professional care, but a reminder that shared experience can be its own kind of support.

    See our community
